Placement and Student Skill Management Portal

A centralized academic placement portal for students and the Training & Placement Cell to manage student academic records, technical and soft skills, projects, certifications, campus placement drives, rule-based eligibility checks, and transparent skill gap analysis.

Student Sign In Register as Student T&P Admin Sign In

Core Capabilities

  • Student profile with CGPA, branch, semester, and backlog tracking
  • Technical skills, soft skills, projects, and certifications management
  • Automated backend eligibility verification per placement drive
  • Rule-based skill match percentage and clear skill gap identification
  • Real-time application tracking from Applied to Selected
  • Admin management for students, companies, drives, and reports

For Students

Maintain your academic portfolio, verify eligibility before applying, inspect missing skills against company requirements, and track application status updates.

For Placement Cell (Admin)

Register recruiting companies, publish placement drives with CGPA, backlog, and branch rules, shortlist applicants, and review branch-wise placement reports.

Transparent Rule Engine

Eligibility and skill matching run on the Express backend using deterministic rules and MongoDB persistence with zero mock data.
